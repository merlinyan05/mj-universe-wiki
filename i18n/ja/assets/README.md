## 初期生成プロンプト

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: photographic hero cover for a community open-source MJ Universe fan-fiction setting wiki on GitHub. Create a polished, humorous cinematic photograph, landscape 16:9, full-bleed, immediately readable at README width. Scene: a sunlit Chinese high-school classroom after class, wood desks, worn green chalkboard, warm window light, subtle purple and gold accents. Three adult fictional Marvel characters share one desk in a candid, believable group interaction: in the middle Thanos in his familiar gold-and-dark armor is earnestly writing in an open thick setting encyclopedia; on his left, small Rocket Raccoon stands on the adjacent desk and holds up a smartphone for Iron Man to see; on his right, Iron Man with red-gold armor and raised faceplate leans toward Rocket, trying to suppress a mischievous grin. Their eyes and gestures must connect: Rocket and Iron Man are sharing secret information and exchanging a knowing look, while Thanos notices them with a proud but embarrassed sideways glance. All three remain in the SAME coherent classroom space, natural body scale, hands and paws anatomically clear, no isolated portrait panels. The open encyclopedia and several sticky notes visually suggest shared lore, character relationships and parallel universes. On the chalkboard behind them, a tasteful hand-drawn branching network of stars, circles and connected character silhouettes hints at multiple universes and community contributions; keep these abstract with no illegible small writing. Composition: generous clear upper third for beautiful legible title typography integrated naturally with the chalkboard, group interaction in middle/lower two-thirds, no cropped faces. Exact title in large ivory chalk-like editorial lettering: 'MJ UNIVERSE WIKI'. Exact single Chinese subtitle beneath it: '全网首个开源 MJ 宇宙设定集'. Only these two text lines; render Chinese characters accurately. Style: high-end cinematic live-action fan-comedy still, tactile skin and fur, realistic armor reflections, warm inviting colors, detailed but uncluttered, funny expressions rather than an epic battle pose. No Marvel studio logo, no platform watermark, no usernames, no QR code, no extra captions, no unrelated characters.
```
