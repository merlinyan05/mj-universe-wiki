# Parents' name chain

"Who is so-and-so" looks like a question, but in fact it repeats a name that has already been exposed. Commenters then extend the family to parents, descendants and grandparents, and also investigate the information provider. [R02](../sources/R02.md), [R06](../sources/R06.md), [R07](../sources/R07.md), [R10](../sources/R10.md), [R13](../sources/R13.md)

Names are maintained in the [character files](../characters/README.md); [candidates with unclear relationships](../relationships/待核对姓名.md) are not forced into a family tree.
