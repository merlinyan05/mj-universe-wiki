# "Now we're really in trouble"

Usually said between Iron Man and Rocket Raccoon as a warning, a tease or schadenfreude: the person addressed is not necessarily the one about to suffer. The escalation that follows makes the warning come true.

- Evidence status: the usage comes from the editorial proposal [E01](../sources/E01.md), and [S5](../sources/S5.md) provides examples of cooperation and escalation; the frequency of "usually" has not been counted.
- When recording a new example: add who says it to whom, whose consequences are foretold, and the in-video timestamp, rather than quoting a single line.
