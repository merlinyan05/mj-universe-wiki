# R53: Fake top-student answers and the habit callback to the next subject

- Verification date: 2026-11-09.
- Actual scope: public posting information, audio transcription assisting a full read-through, spot checks of key frames/subtitles and a few public comments; not watched shot by shot in full or read through all comments.

## Source and public credit

- [Thanos frantically checks answers with people after every exam; now he is really in trouble](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1gX4i6oEbu/): 西伯利亚冬泳怪鸽, 2026-11-06 11:55:07 (UTC+08:00), 111 seconds.

## Observation and positioning

Thanos keeps checking answers with classmates, and a teasing friend falsely claims one set of answers came from the top student Superman next door. It briefly looks as if the lie will not work, then the official answers and the news of Superman's absence invalidate the false authority; after apologizing, Thanos asks about the next subject, and the character habit returns at the end.

## Entries supported

[Fake systems and false authority](../universes/校园宇宙/patterns/伪系统与假权威答案.md)

## Usage boundaries

"Superman the top student" appears inside the lie and is not enough by itself to prove Superman's real grades in that world. The order of answer source, exposure and ending is kept, and the lie is not treated as fact.
