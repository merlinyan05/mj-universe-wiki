## Observation and positioning

About 00:04, the Ancient One makes Doctor Strange temporary class monitor and record the classmates who were talking; a classmate objects and is recorded again. Later the classmates retell the family names to the teacher by speaking up for the Doctor, and the disciplinary procedure instead enlarges the joke.

Key subtitles checked: about 01:48 father "Qi Ba" (奇巴), 01:53 mother "Wife-Watcher" (妻管严, literally "wife-control-strict", a pun on henpecked), 01:56 grandfather "Gasoline" (汽油), 01:57 grandmother "Starter" (启动器). These are teasing terms with clear in-video referents, and not all necessarily correspond to family members who actually appear.
