- Verification date: 2026-11-09.
- Actual scope: public posting information, audio transcription assisting a full read-through, spot checks of key frames/subtitles and a few public comments; not watched shot by shot in full or read through all comments.

## Source and public credit

- [[mj universe prequel] The origin of the poodle "I'll wait for you to come back"](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yLnV6wEYv/): 只因寄太美哦, 2026-10-18 23:16:00 (UTC+08:00), 345 seconds.

## Observation and positioning

The story tells the life of the pet Xiaobai: helping rescue a child, a family misfortune, being sold, and waiting for the old owner. The rescue involves police; the fact that the same creator also posts development-history videos is not a reason to treat the prequel narration as the actual process by which the real meme arose.
