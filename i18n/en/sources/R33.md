# R33: Kerry Callen's 2010 parody cover record

- Platform / public creator: Kerry Callen's personal blog.
- Original text: [What if DC published Marvel characters in the 1960's?](https://kerrycallen.blogspot.com/2010/11/what-if-dc-published-marvel-characters.html)
- Readable entry: [the author's 2010 archive](https://kerrycallen.blogspot.com/2010/), the November 1 entry.
- Page date: 2010-11-01 (the blog's original time zone); verification date: 2026-11-08.
- Actual scope: the title, body and date in the author's archive; the standalone article entry failed to load this round. The cited image was also seen at about 03:00–03:55 in [R28](R28.md); the original page's full image files were not checked directly.

## What it supports and boundaries

The author explicitly describes imagining DC publishing Marvel characters in the style of the time and making the corresponding covers. This is an original-site record of parody creation; the 1960s in the title cannot be taken as the actual publication decade, nor can it be called official Marvel canon.

R28 places it in the prehistory of Fat Spider-Man images. This round can only directly verify the creation statement and the date; it is not claimed to be the earliest instance of the image site-wide, nor is a direct inheritance to modern Chinese videos asserted. Only the text link is kept; no permission to repost the images has been obtained.
