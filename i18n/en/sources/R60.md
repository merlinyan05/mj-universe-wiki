## Observation and positioning

The MJ version of Thanos enters another world and treats Hulk and others according to his own acquaintance understanding, which the other side does not necessarily accept. About 03:01, the Ancient One explicitly says the visitor does not belong to her universe and that she is not his teacher; the corresponding bilingual subtitles were checked.

Sharing appearance, name and abilities does not mean teacher-student or CP relationships are automatically inherited. A visitor carries their original experience, but local relationships must be checked separately.
