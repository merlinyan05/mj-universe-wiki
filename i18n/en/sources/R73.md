## Observation and positioning

In the creator's snowy-day works, the armor provides heating and offline movies, and companions warm themselves around it; in the floor-sleeping episode, about 01:49–01:53 they ask Thor for a power bank, which is not verified lightning-based generation. In the horror-film episode, about 00:12–00:16 it is explained that under the blizzard the base station only supports calls, and technology does not restore the network out of nowhere; 02:33–02:36 Loki comes to borrow a power bank. Rocket first urges showing a horror film, then when the raccoon killer is proposed, objects on the grounds that they should not scare their roommate.

In the hot pot episode, about 02:26–02:40 tasks such as handing out bowls and chopsticks and shopping are assigned; about 02:49–03:03 Rocket picks the tangyuan and insists on his own taste. About 03:13–03:37 Hulk crushes the iron basin and hides it after kneading it into a ball. The November 10 video opens by mentioning last night's tangyuan, and about 01:28–01:36 asks about the same iron basin, forming a concrete prop-and-action continuity.

In the later work, 00:42 calls him the American class monitor, and 01:58.8–01:59.6 Captain America calls himself class monitor and admits he did not lead by example. The teacher asks level by level, and the students first give up matters such as online classes and hot pot; about 01:48–02:00 companions rush to take responsibility. About 02:09–02:16 the teacher says the guard team captain is Rocket's family member, without stating a name or specific kinship direction.

A threat of possible jail or expulsion is not the same as having been imprisoned or expelled; after saying he quits 501 he still interacts with companions, and an official dormitory move is not confirmed.
