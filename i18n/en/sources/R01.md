# R01: A parents'-name chain on Weibo

- Recorded: 2026-11-01

- Source: [A fun roundup of the parents' names of the superheroes](https://m.weibo.cn/2/detail/5359090488049750), Junehook, page dated October 28.
- Verification: The body of the public post is readable; most entries give a father and a mother in pairs, while the Hulk line omits the kinship label on the second name, and the record keeps that ambiguity. See the kinship name variants in the [character files](../characters/README.md).
- Value: It fills in mothers' names and shows a naming style that mixes everyday names, objects and homophones.
- Boundary: This is one netizen's single chain and cannot be written up as an official list; it also lists the Green Giant and the Hulk as separate entries, and the record must preserve that feature of the source.
