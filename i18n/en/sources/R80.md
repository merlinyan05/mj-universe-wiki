## Observation and positioning

In 奶蛙帮's 67 extreme-cold episodes, Rocket reports supermarket anomalies, and Iron Man contacts family, arranges shelter and purchasing funds; in the first video, about 01:30–01:35, the Doctor proposes freezing space and time to keep things fresh, and the second video opens by reviewing the related purchases. The plot then advances through severe cold and bringing people into the shelter.

Hayakawa-Ak1's doomsday campus episode 5 instead advances around heating, electricity, fuel, water, gathering for warmth and danger while outside. The same snow scene does not mean the same disaster, and the daily snowy-day online-class dormitory series cannot be merged in; school status also does not mean all abilities fail.
