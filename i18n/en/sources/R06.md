# R06: A roundup of fathers' names, comment images and education homophones

- Recorded: 2026-11-01

- Source: [Meet all the superheroes' fathers in one go](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1AGxg6KEAL/).
- Verification: The comments were browsed directly, and the text image attached to the first top comment was actually viewed; the image lists ten rows of characters and name arrows, with Doctor Strange appearing under two name versions. When sampling the video, the subtitles also state explicitly that Ant-Man's father is Ant Duyong (蚁大勇).
- Observation: The everyday-name chain in the image coexists with homophone chains in the comments such as "Qi's dad", "Strange the Master" and "Star Rail". Under Thor, the replies stack the original father Odin and Lei Yuanchao (雷援朝) into one "Chinese name" joke.
- Usable canon: The list contains several naming mechanisms, and a character having two names is no reason to delete one; correcting the source material can itself become a running gag.
- Boundary: The comment image only has arrows; reading it as a fathers' list depends on the subject of the video, and an untitled image alone cannot establish each generation's kinship.
