## Observation and positioning

Thanos buys two ordinary raccoons, Rocket demands they be released, and the ending reveals the kinship through the homophone of "driftwood" (浮木) and "parents" (父母); the frames do not separately mark the identity or names of the two. The title, speech context and comment interpretation correspond to each other, but "driftwood" cannot be taken as a proper name.

Root comment 323419882352 continues that Rocket sells them first and then asks for them back, and a reply says they can be sold again; the original video does not confirm this transaction. Root comment 319668646209 gives another prank explanation that is not kinship and does not override the in-video context either. It is recorded separately from the kinship name comments in R75.
