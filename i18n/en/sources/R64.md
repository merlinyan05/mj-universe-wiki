# R64: The same-name Lingguang contest and the pending boundary of tool attribution

- Verification date: 2026-11-09.
- Source: ["Lingguang · New Frame" contest official site](https://www.xinshijueai.com/), [2026-10-23 government account report](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34315196), [2026-11-02 retrospective article](https://www.sohu.com/a/1095957077_258858).
- Actual scope: the official site's organizer information and event visual date, the government body text, the retrospective body text; no official original page that independently corresponds to "the Ant app's October 8 event" was obtained.

## What can be confirmed and what is still pending

The official contest site lists organizer information such as Xining-related government units and China Youth Daily, and the event visuals and government report point to a September 26 launch. The retrospective article links an October 8 event with cash and computing-power rewards to the Ant Lingguang app.

The names and prize figures are close but not enough to conclude it is the same organizer or event; the materials are not stitched into a definite causal claim that "the app's contest on some day created the entire MJ boom". Even if the app event's original page were found, whether the works' spread was driven by the contest would still need independent material.

[R61](R61.md) has separate October 6–7 original posts and the Lingguang watermark, which support that earlier works existed and do not in turn prove that all specific event claims are wrong.
