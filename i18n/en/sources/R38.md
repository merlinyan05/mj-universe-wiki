## What can be confirmed

This provides reporting from the same period, independent of the autumn meme explainers, and at least confirms that such a report existed by August 4.

Lightning News explicitly credits Red Star News; the two are not two independent interviews. The exact launch and shutdown dates of the effect were not verified, nor was it tested whether it could still be triggered in November 2026; conflicting AI-generated summaries are not used to fill in dates.
