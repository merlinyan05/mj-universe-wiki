## Observation and positioning

In the racing video, about 00:20–00:30, companions notice Ant-Man's car appears but he himself is absent, and ask where he went; this video does not reveal the location and it is not automatically filled in as the usual place.

Outing episode 2 follows up on apology after pranks, compensation through an outing and group activities, and ends unable to find Hulk and Ant-Man, creating suspense for the next search; there is no evidence that the two must be in the same place or at the old meme location. Absence can both let the audience complete the joke tacitly and genuinely drive a search in the plot.
