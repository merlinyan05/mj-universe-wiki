- Verification date: 2026-11-10.

## Source and public credit

- [Thanos brings a phone to school and is caught by the teacher; now he is really in trouble](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Dvn86WEuB/): 只因寄太美哦, 2026-10-23 21:40:43 (UTC+08), 153 seconds.

## Observation and positioning

In the first half, the original phone owner is the red-and-blue Spider-Man called MJ, and the person cannot be recorded wrongly just because the title says Thanos. Companions trade usage rights with promises not to tell and offers of charging spots, and the original owner ends up with little play time.

In the second half, everyone brings a phone back at school; the Doctor is refused when borrowing, then the teacher finds the phone, and the ending has the Doctor taking revenge by not letting others play. This creator's revenge-reporting motive can be recorded without establishing that every Doctor always tells. The teacher is an ordinary female figure and is not automatically called the Ancient One; Spider-Man's body-type jump does not directly prove a transformation ability.
