## Observation and positioning

Classmates argue over queuing for and time limits on a coin-operated hair dryer: Thanos's bald head, the raccoon's amount of hair and the Doctor's ability to retrieve coins each enter the conflict and the trades. The characters have different needs, calculations and counter-measures, and the resource becomes a shared goal for multi-person interaction.
