## Observation and positioning

In 恶の妈抱's crossing episode 5, from about 03:06 the companions bring an apology to find Thanos and are attacked; at about 03:44–03:50 the Doctor points out that they have the wrong universe. In episode 6, about 01:40–01:47, JARVIS judges from the local Doctor and Hulk still being in the building that the same-face figure outside comes from another universe.

Episode 5 about 01:04–01:08 involves gauntlet face recognition, and episode 6 about 00:58 explains that a move failed because the enemy Thanos had not eaten breakfast; both are conditional jokes of this work. Hao Zhiqiang is directly called Hulk, which does not mean the father is really present; the same face also does not inherit acquaintance relationships.

The new-classmate short has a Buzz Lightyear-style armored figure enter the classroom, and the voice corrects the teacher who misnames him Bamasiguang. Cross-IP same-class presentation can be recorded, but cross-universe travel is not explained; no real character identity is guessed from homophones or face shape, and no permanent alias is established.
