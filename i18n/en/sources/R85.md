The August 31 explainer provides a node showing that MJ meme discussion already existed at the time; its chapter summary is not used to prove spread causality. The November explainer's original page chapter points are marked as AI-generated and cannot be taken as the creator's verbatim speech or as evidence for first creation, bans and other historical facts.
