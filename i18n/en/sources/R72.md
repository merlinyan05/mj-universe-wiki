## Observation and positioning

The August 26 work mixes a bear head with red-and-blue Spider-Man clothing into a group AI image; the title mentions the MJ easter egg and the page has an AI-generated label. September 11 is a group of round, fat Spider-Men eating, with a visible Lingguang AI generation watermark and creator statement; no 67 Diner sign is seen. On September 26, fat Spider-Man interacts with Thanos and the Infinity Gauntlet, with the Lingguang watermark.

The three provide early mixed-style, Fat Spider-Man and Thanos-interaction posting nodes, earlier than the October samples in R61. Original posting, first creation or direct inheritance between them is not confirmed; current popularity does not represent popularity at posting time. "They say the easter egg is gone" in the title is also not a platform operations announcement.

One comment on the September 11 video plays a hunter, and the creator replies that they were derelict; this is comment-creator play and does not mean a hunt happened in the video. An August 26 comment exaggerates the tool's promotional years and is not used as product age or event history.
