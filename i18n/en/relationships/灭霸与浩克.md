# The Thanos and Hulk CP version

- Scope: a continuous CP series by some creators; not a fixed relationship in every branch.
- Basis: [R03](../sources/R03.md) shows the titles and compilation of episodes 1-4 of the series, plus extended titles such as Doctor Strange witnessing it and borrowing money; [R11](../sources/R11.md) is a lead from a fitness video example.
- What can be confirmed: a fan version that keeps using the relationship between the two exists.
- Follow-up verification: [R76](../sources/R76.md) has now verified the partner and family terms in the Crazy Yuanyuan series, see the section below; the earlier title-verification scope is retained.
- Comment expansion: the earlier family speculation based only on Gamora's appearance is still comment co-creation; newly added in-video daughter/mother terms do not mean a specific blood relationship mechanism has been proven.

Different relationships from other creators can be added side by side, with the source and the actual viewing scope noted.

## Relationships across universes cannot be inherited automatically

## 2026-11-10 verification: partner and daily family life

Episode 2 of [R76](../sources/R76.md) has shared washing up, feeding and companionship; episode 3 has holding a baby in the hospital, partner terms and the address "my girl"; episode 4 at 00:01 talks to [Gamora](../characters/卡魔拉.md) about "your mom", with the three in the same frame. This creator's family portrayal can be recorded and supports Thanos and Hulk taking on parental roles.

No registered marriage, specific birth/adoption mechanism or original-work relationship is confirmed on this basis, and it is not spliced with another creator's adult family in [R83](../sources/R83.md). [R82](../sources/R82.md) giving Hulk cake for free only supports that this video shows favouritism and does not separately prove that the same relationship stage has been reached.
