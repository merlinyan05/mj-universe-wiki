# MJ Universe Wiki | Characters, Relationships, Worldlines and Classic Memes

**MJ Universe Wiki · the first open-source MJ universe reference on the internet. Everyone who enjoys the joke is welcome to help complete the characters, personalities, relationships, worldlines and story formulas!**

Who is Mie Jianguo? Where did Ant-Man go? Who says "Now we're really in trouble" to whom?

`mj-universe-wiki` collects the character personalities, parents' names, family trees, classic memes and comment-section co-creation from MJ-universe / Marvel fan-made short videos on Bilibili, Douyin and other platforms. It covers the campus, standard-universe chaos, everyday-life and Thanos/Hulk CP versions, so you can look up settings, write scripts and make videos — and help improve this community wiki.

> The "first open-source" claim is based on a public search up to 2026-11-01: no earlier dedicated open-source reference on the same theme was found. This does not exhaust every platform or unindexed project. If you know an earlier one, please add and correct it through an Issue. [Search record](docs/MJ_RESEARCH_NOTES.md)

Different creators and individual episodes may have different versions. This reference keeps the differences and their sources instead of declaring a single canon for every work. Character relationships, relatives' names and plot mechanics are recorded with their known scope; corrections and further evidence are welcome.

This repository explains characters, relationships, worldlines and classic memes through text and images. Images support specific entries or present the wiki: covers, character identification images, relationship charts, necessary screenshots, and short GIFs where motion is needed to explain the point. Generic reaction images, wallpapers and sticker collections are introduced through a text index and external links, with the full asset packs kept outside the repository.

Material is mainly Markdown and JSON and can be downloaded, cloned and read on its own; supported image formats are JPG/JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP. ## How to use it for scripts and videos

1. Check character positioning, personality, relationships and behavioral rules in the [character files](characters/README.md), and choose the version you need.
2. Use [universes and story patterns](universes/README.md) to fix the world rules and how the plot advances; shared memes, cameos and special versions are marked separately.
3. Follow the [source records](sources/README.md) linked from each entry to check the original video, comments and version differences before writing dialogue and plot, so relatives, teachers/students and partners are not mixed up.

To check a character's appearance, start from the [appearance reference](../../characters/README.md#外观参考) and follow the links to film, game and fan-design pages. Only external links are provided for now; there is no complete MJ campus three-view set, and the permitted use of linked material follows the source's license — indexing here does not grant authorization.

If a character reaction, relationship detail or plot pattern is still incomplete, add a line of material or open an Issue; new observations from the original videos also help the next creator.

## How the material is organized

- `characters/`: one complete file per character, recording positioning, personality, behavior, relationships and universe differences.
- `universes/`: world rules and story patterns; universe-specific patterns live under their universe, shared patterns are maintained in one place and cross-linked.
- `relationships/` and `memes/`: relationships, name jokes and catchphrases shared across universes.
- `sources/`: original videos, comments, timestamps and the actual scope checked.

Characters are not duplicated per universe; universe differences are written back into the character file, and universe pages link to it. Story patterns can be reused across universes, and cameos must state which character version they use.

## Help complete the settings

**If you only know one name, one personality trait, one cameo or one classic scene, you are welcome to add it.** You do not need to organize a complete worldview at once, and you do not need to write code.

- Have a lead but cannot edit files yet: open an Issue and choose "add a setting or source". Leads without a source are also welcome — mark them "pending verification".
- Found an error or a version conflict: choose "corrections and version differences" and explain which entry differs and how.
- Want to edit directly: open the relevant character file, click edit on GitHub, and follow the prompts to fork, commit and open a Pull Request.
- Familiar with Git: fork → create a branch → commit → push to your fork → open a PR. Regular maintainers may be granted write access and push branches directly in this repository.

You are welcome to add why Rocket Raccoon always knows the news first, how Thanos strikes back, how characters differ across universes, and more details from the original videos and comments. Different versions and evidence for existing claims are also welcome.

See the [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for concrete steps, copyable commands and examples. Each entry is maintained independently, and small changes can be submitted on their own.

## What makes a useful record

"Thanos's father is Mie Jianguo" and "Who is Huan Xiaoli" provide different information: the first states a kinship direction, while the second only proves that a name appeared. Do not invent a family tree from a name's gender or from two people appearing side by side in a title.

In-video canon, creator supplements, comment co-creation, editorial inference and original expansion are marked separately. Initial claims without public direct evidence are listed as [pending proposals](sources/E01.md) and can be revised with new sources.
