# Broad research on the MJ Universe: branches, parents' names and comment co-creation

Date: 2026-11-01. Together with the [reference guide](MJ_UNIVERSE.md), this records the observed scope of original videos, comments and searches.

Latest follow-up: 2026-11-10, adding character behaviour, family forms of address, continuous creator lines and the August–September AI posting milestones; see the last section of this page, the [character files](../characters/README.md), [story patterns](../universes/patterns/README.md) and [origins and spread history](MJ_HISTORY.md). ## Research questions and actual scope

The research covers branches such as classroom campus, pure universe and pure nonsense, with particular attention to the different uses of Hulk and to parents' and other relatives' names continued in comments.

This round directly browsed public comments on Bilibili and Douyin video pages, looked at Bilibili comment images, some replies and series collection titles, and also read one public Weibo parents'-name chain. The [source index](../sources/README.md) lists 14 R-numbered entries, of which R02 and R14 are new comment observations added to existing sources, and the rest are additions from this round. The sample is purposive, not random, and cannot be used to calculate the share of a name or character trait across the internet.

Douyin search pages require login, but some direct video pages show public comments; what was actually used were the readable direct video pages. No login, no commenting and no change of follow state. Some pages show only a limited number of comments and cannot be treated as having read every reply. Bilibili titles, comments, image text and spot-checked video frames are labelled separately, and content not watched in full is not written up as "watched in full".
