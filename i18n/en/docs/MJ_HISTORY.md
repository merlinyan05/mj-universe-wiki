# The origins and spread of the MJ Universe

Date updated and checked: 2026-11-10. This page records the real-world spread of the works and memes, not the characters' in-story backgrounds or an in-universe timeline.

For now it is more accurate to understand the MJ Universe as **a community fan collection in which several older memes, images and AI short dramas gradually converge**, rather than a single unified setting written once by one person. This is an editorial judgement based on the material below; the sole originator of Chinese MJ videos, the campus line or the name "Mie Jianguo" is still unconfirmed.

## Stages proposed by retrospectives, and remaining disputes

The narrative in R08 at about 00:42—03:23 is: early Fat Spider-Man shorts, then combining with food and drink, circus/clown and 67 Diner material, continuing to absorb old memes, and expanding into Avengers gags and campus. It can be used to understand how the subject matter came together, but the months, priority and causal spread in it still lack segment-by-segment original-post evidence.

A popular comment under R29 proposes another remembered order: after Fat Spider-Man there are also 不烧心, the horror poodle, the Joker, the 67 Diner and kung fu, then the Avengers expansion. It adds search directions; one comment cannot overturn other creators, nor be stitched into a verified precise chronology.

The following claims are not yet written as history:

- **A concentrated platform crackdown caused the turn to campus**: retrospectives describe takedowns, throttling and a creative turn; no corresponding platform notice or complete work sample has been obtained, so the scale, cause and single causality cannot be confirmed.
- **A certain tool/competition created the whole craze**: tools, events and creative incentives mentioned in videos or comments only prove that such an explanation exists; official event material and spread data are missing.
- **All versions come from one video**: Chinese MJ, the Fat Spider-Man images, Mie Jianguo's father name, the old Ant-Man meme and the campus pattern are different open questions and cannot share one "founding ancestor" conclusion.
- **When the effect launched/was taken offline**: retrospectives and aggregator pages give late July, early August and various takedown dates; for now only the contemporary news record of the play style is confirmed, and no precise operating calendar is assembled. [R38](../sources/R38.md)
- **MJ is short for Midjourney**: this round's material is not enough to support explaining a generation tool's name as a settled etymology for the community's name.
- **Mary Jane and Michelle Jones are the same name**: retrospectives and comments express this differently. When a specific film version is involved the character name should be checked; two names sharing an abbreviation are not merged into one person, nor is Fat Spider-Man inferred to be the MJ of the films. [R28](../sources/R28.md), [R34](../sources/R34.md)

## Addition 2026-11-09: tools, same-named competitions and fictional prequels

[R64](../sources/R64.md) compares the official "Lingguang · Xin Zhen" competition, government reports and a retrospective article linking it to the Ant app. The organiser information and event dates on the official site cannot be merged into the app event on the strength of a name or a prize figure alone; the specific correspondence still needs the official page, and how the event contributed to spread must be proven separately. The record of works on 6—7 October in R61 is again earlier than the 8 October milestone mentioned in retrospectives, so it is not written as one proven causal chain.

A visible Lingguang watermark can prove that a film carries a tool mark; it cannot be inferred backwards that all MJ videos were generated by the same tool. [R49](../sources/R49.md) has another example marked updream; visible marks are recorded here, and a watermark is not automatically equated with a complete production process.

Entries for old material have also been located separately: the title/channel of Marvel's official promo can be looked up, but the original was not watched in full this round; the 2017 Ant-Man discussion and the August chat-animation recreation are both in [R69](../sources/R69.md). That old material exists, later captions and the formation of campus character traits are different questions.

The Xiaobai story in [R65](../sources/R65.md) is a pet backstory written by the creator and belongs to an in-universe prequel. Even if the same creator also made a development history, it cannot serve as the real-world origin of the poodle meme or as a real rescue event.

## How to keep adding evidence

Contributions of **earlier original posts** are welcome: link, public author credit, publication time, whether it is original/repost/archive, the relevant time point, and exactly how much earlier it is than the current material. Comments can be leads; attach context and mark them "comment recollection". Creator self-reports and actually visible works are recorded separately. Several explainers citing the same source do not count as several independent pieces of evidence.

New material goes into [sources](../sources/README.md) first, then this page is updated; character traits, relationships and play styles are still maintained in [characters](../characters/README.md), [memes](../memes/README.md) and [universes and story patterns](../universes/README.md). The spread timeline is not treated as a plot canon shared by all parallel worlds.
