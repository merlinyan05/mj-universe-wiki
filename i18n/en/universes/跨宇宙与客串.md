# Cross-universe, inherited memories and cameos

Characters are still maintained once each in the [character files](../characters/README.md). Below, the ways of entry actually used in the video examples are separated, so that subject matter, stage and creator differences are not all mechanically split into new universes.

| Situation | Verified video examples | What should be recorded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An outside visitor enters the MJ world | The serious Thanos of [R58](../sources/R58.md) arrives in the MJ version | Where they come from, whether local characters recognise them, how the misidentification affects their actions. |
| A local character gains memories of another world | [R59](../sources/R59.md) | Whether body and local identity are retained; memory does not automatically mean the characters have swapped. |
| An MJ character goes the other way into another world | [R60](../sources/R60.md) | The visitor carries an understanding of familiar people, but locals need not accept it. At about 03:01 the Ancient One states plainly that she is not his teacher. |
| A character or meme enters another subject matter | The village banquet in [R66](../sources/R66.md) | Which appearance, abilities, forms of address and jokes carry over; the whole campus relationship is not imported automatically. |

## Shared characters do not mean inherited relationships

Teacher-student, parent-child, CP and classmate relationships are confirmed per version. The visitor in R60 treats Hulk as an acquaintance, while the local reaction differs; the visitor's attitude cannot be used to infer a local romance. Asking "who is Mie Jianguo" on its own may be borrowing a meme and is not the only evidence that a crossover happened.

R66 features a purple-gauntleted character, Iron Man, the Joker, Fat Spider-Man, and memes from the 67 Diner and JARVIS; the name Mie Jianguo in the form of address and the appearance must be recorded separately, and a single label cannot be used to decide a parent-child identity directly.

## Scenes, stages, language and creator versions

Classrooms, dorms, day students and sports days can all belong to campus material. The two creators in R56 give different university sequels and are recorded as their own arrangements; switching to Cantonese does not automatically constitute a new world. The fake system in R52 is a prank device built by classmates, and a real system world is not established just because the title says "system".

A character's in-fiction backstory is also kept apart from the real-world origin: the Xiaobai story in R65 belongs to the work's past; for the real-world spread see [the history of the meme](../docs/MJ_HISTORY.md), and no meme's real birth is inferred backwards from a prequel.

## When adding a cameo

Please include the creator, the original link, the time point, the distinction between visitor and local, and which relationship is actually carried over; whatever is not stated stays unclear. Video examples, creator statements and comment role-play are labelled separately; a comment mentioning an Ultraman in the next class does not mean he has already appeared on screen. ## How characters with the same face are recognised (example added 2026-11-10)

Episode 5 of the crossover in [R81](../sources/R81.md) has the companions bring an apology to the wrong Thanos; only after they are attacked does the doctor point out that this is not their companion. In episode 6 JARVIS judges that the one outside is a visitor because the local character with the same face is still inside the tower. Recognition requires in-video information; characters do not become omniscient without explanation just because the audience recognises the appearance.

The gauntlet's face recognition and the failure of a move caused by not eating breakfast are conditions of a single episode only, not shared ability rules for every universe. Calling a companion by a parent's name directly also does not mean the parent is actually present.

R81 also has a short in which a Buzz Lightyear-like figure appears as a new classmate, supporting a cross-IP class cameo, without explaining any cross-universe travel. A teacher calling out the wrong name also does not make it a new character's permanent alias.

For how subject matter and stages are organised see [the campus creator series](校园宇宙/作者系列.md); snow, age or a jump in AI models do not each justify a new universe.
