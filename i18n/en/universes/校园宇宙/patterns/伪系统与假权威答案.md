# Fake systems and false authoritative answers

- Type: a mechanism summary of campus-exclusive video examples; when other universes reuse it, the source of authority, available technology and relationships must be verified separately.
- Characters: [Thanos](../../../characters/灭霸.md), [Iron Man](../../../characters/钢铁侠.md), [JARVIS](../../../characters/贾维斯.md), [Doctor Strange](../../../characters/奇异博士.md).
- Basis: [R52](../../../sources/R52.md), [R53](../../../sources/R53.md), with transcript-assisted reading and spot checks of key frames.

## Variant one: a fake system luring him on

A teasing friend builds a system behind the scenes → the protagonist believes he has been given a unique ability / reward → points and quest feedback lure him into continuing → the performance escalates → the grand prize does not match expectations → the prank is exposed and consequences are borne.

In R52 Iron Man, JARVIS and the doctor take part in the device and the situation, and Thanos acts according to the quests. "The system exists" and "what the system says is true" are kept apart: in this video it is a hoax built by classmates, and a real system universe is not established automatically.

## Variant two: a false authoritative answer

The protagonist checks answers again and again → a companion packages the answer as something a top student provides → it looks credible for a moment / the deception does not hold → the official answer or an absent fact exposes the source → apology and compensation → the protagonist asks about the next subject, and the habit becomes the closing callback.

R53 treats Superman next door as the authority on answers, but he is a label inside the deception; this alone cannot establish a verified top-student trait for Superman.

## Shared mechanism and boundaries

Both use an authority the protagonist believes in to set up an information gap, but the task performance, the post-exam anxiety and the way it is exposed are different. A summary does not mean every work uses all the steps, and a teasing friend does not automatically have unlimited technology. It can cross-reference [pranks and counter-pranks](../../patterns/整蛊与反制.md) without duplicating a separate character setting.
