# Competition for public resources and trading of abilities

- Type: campus-exclusive video examples; it can be borrowed for diners, public facilities and other everyday settings, with the characters' identities verified separately.
- Characters: [Thanos](../../../characters/灭霸.md), [Rocket Raccoon](../../../characters/小浣熊.md), [Doctor Strange](../../../characters/奇异博士.md) and other classmates.
- Basis: [R54](../../../sources/R54.md), the audio and key frames of the coin-operated hairdryer video example.

## How it unfolds

Limited facilities and queuing rules → each person has a different calculation about who may use it, how long it takes and what it costs → physical traits or superpowers affect the allocation → someone offers technology in exchange for payment / takes advantage → the group re-coordinates or counter-attacks.

In R54 the bald head, the raccoon's amount of fur and the spell that retrieves the coin enter the use conflict of the same facility. Characters competing for the same goal at the same time, with the waiter, the user and the trader affecting one another, builds group relationships better than delivering unrelated lines one after another.

## Boundaries

This is a summary of this video's mechanism, and specific fees, spell costs and body jokes are not written up as permanent world rules. Using the same kind of facility does not require opening a new universe; when changing the setting, first state the rules and who knows them.

## How a shared phone becomes an imbalance of distribution

The first half of [R77](../../../sources/R77.md): Spider-Man originally owns the phone → trades the right to use it for secrecy and a charging spot → companions take turns borrowing it → the original owner in turn cannot play. The second half: everyone brings a phone → the doctor is refused when he borrows one → the teacher checks the phones → the doctor's payback is not letting them play. The reporting has a motive within that episode and is not fixed as every creator's personality.

In the snowy-day dormitory of [R73](../../../sources/R73.md) the resources also include suit heating, offline films and a power bank; the hot pot has companions divide the shopping. Characters each have resources or tasks, and only through cooperation does the plot move forward. Thor handing over a power bank does not mean his divine power generating electricity has been verified. In [R82](../../../sources/R82.md) game labour in exchange for a treat is also a variant of a debt of favours and the right to use something.
