# Late-night emo, laughed at the next day

- Typical universe: [Campus Universe](../README.md).
- Cross-universe reuse: it can be transplanted to other groups of acquaintances, but the setting and the characters' identities must be stated again; reuse does not automatically make them classmates.

- Type: a summary of plot mechanisms; not every original video contains every step.
- Basis: [S1](../../../sources/S1.md), [S2](../../../sources/S2.md); [early video examples and time ranges](../../../../../references/mj-emo-formulas.json).

1. **Genuinely sad**: Thanos posts seriously at night, and the copy contains one concrete word or action that can be seized on.
2. **A private scene first**: Rocket Raccoon finds or obtains the information, and reads, comments on, questions and discusses it with Iron Man.
3. **Warning in advance**: a judgement such as "now we're really in trouble" appears between the two, and the audience knows something is coming tomorrow.
4. **Applied to his face the next day**: in the classroom, corridor, canteen and other everyday settings, last night's words become a nickname, a prop or an actual action.
5. **The reaction keeps producing gags**: Thanos' excuses or pleas for help bring out another layer of information that Rocket has been keeping.
6. **The consequences land**: parents, teachers, chat evidence or a new audience make the earlier warning come true. It can also end on a breakdown or a new round of posting.

The fast pace comes from tight line-pickups, quick information escalation and reactions that pay off. Dark humour can be harsher, but it has to grow out of character leverage, counter-pranks and relationships; unrelated gags must not replace causality, and everyone must not speak in the same foul-mouthed register.

## 2026-11-09 video example: an ordinary question picked up step by step

[R50](../../../sources/R50.md) first establishes that Thanos has blocked Iron Man; at about 00:23-00:31 it explicitly arranges for JARVIS to obtain the content, so there is an in-video bridge explaining how he knows the copy the next day. Then Iron Man asks why the phone does not show the month, Rocket shifts to the doctor's time ability, and the doctor lands on Thanos' renaming copy. The three take on asking, redirecting and exposing respectively, and only then does an ordinary question gradually take on a teasing edge.

This supports the variant "the copy is broken into everyday questions → partners chain the lines → the protagonist sees through it", and does not require reading the whole copy out every time. Who can see a blocked post and why must be explained per episode; [misjudged parent accounts](误评家长账号与用语回流.md) is a different misunderstanding and cannot be spliced in as the same cause.

Some comments find the blessing at the end of R50 abrupt; [friendship payback](集体补救与友情回扣.md) has video examples of remedy and relationship action. Different endings and limited feedback are listed side by side, and no single ending is prescribed for the whole internet.

## An unblocked reposter and bait updates

At about 01:12-01:18 of [R75](../../../sources/R75.md) it is established that Ant-Man reposted, and Iron Man points out that Thanos forgot to block Ant-Man. This is a traceable relay of information, recorded separately from JARVIS obtaining it and from misjudged parent accounts; one cannot write only that everyone still knew after the block while omitting the bridge.

[R78](../../../sources/R78.md) has another version where he posts an update on purpose and pretends to be angry the next day to trade for spicy strips, and the plan finally works. It exploits the habit of a teasing friend who normally laughs and then apologises, and belongs to bait updates; the premise differs from the genuinely sad pattern at the start of this page, so it is not rewritten as all emo being a scheme, and no failure ending is added out of thin air.
