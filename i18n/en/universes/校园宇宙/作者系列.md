# Creator series and world conditions in campus material

Characters are still maintained once each in the [character files](../../characters/README.md). This page indexes continuous stories and the resource conditions behind them; it does not assign a single universe number to every creator online, and one creator need not share a single world line across all works.

## Xiaoxin the blogger: the snowy-dorm series

| Stage of the work | What carries over and what has been verified | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snowball fight | Heavy snow disrupts classes; the armour's heating exposes the bluff about not feeling the cold | [R73](../../sources/R73.md) |
| Sleeping on the floor, horror films | Huddling around the armour for warmth, borrowing a power bank, watching offline films after the network drops; the visiting Loki is mistaken for a scare | R73 |
| Hot-pot shopping | The companions split the work; Raccoon insists on tangyuan; Hulk damages the iron basin and hides it | R73 |
| Accounting the next day | The opening calls back to the tangyuan, the teacher traces the same iron basin, friends compete to take responsibility, and Captain America calls himself class monitor | R73 |

"501" comes from the dorm number used in dialogue. The iron basin and the tangyuan provide concrete continuous evidence; videos cannot be stitched together merely because the creator is the same. Jail or expulsion is a threat or a possibility, not an ending; the line about leaving 501 does not by itself prove a formal room change. The class monitor, the food preferences and the family resources are written back into the character files separately.

| Creator series | Verified world conditions | What is not merged with other versions |

| Naiwabang's 67 extreme-cold arc | Rocket reports the supply anomaly, Iron Man arranges funding and shelter, the doctor uses space and time suspension to keep things fresh, and people are later brought in to stay | How resources, technology and spells are available in this series; not automatically true of ordinary campus |

Basis: [R80](../../sources/R80.md). Snow does not by itself decide the world category; an everyday snowy day still means online classes and playing in the snow, which differs from the conditions of a disaster serial.

## Graduation and grown-up sequels

[R56](../../sources/R56.md) has two creators giving different university arrangements; the hand-drawn "Later" in [R83](../../sources/R83.md) extends further into design work, shelved painting, family and the dinner table. These are futures each creator wrote; they do not form one shared life timeline for all classmates, nor do they merge partners and children with the [Thanos/Hulk family serial](../灭霸与绿巨人宇宙/README.md).
