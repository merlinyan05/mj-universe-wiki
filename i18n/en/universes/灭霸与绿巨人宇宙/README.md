# Thanos & Hulk Universe

- Common scenes: romance, bodybuilding, misunderstandings, relationship reveals, crossover mash-ups
- World rules: records the creator versions built around the relationship between these two; individual series may still differ in progress and in who is close to whom, so they are not merged into one continuous storyline.
- Version boundaries: universe categories can be layered and are not official universe numbers; each creator's version is recorded separately.

Thanos/Hulk already has continuous CP-series titles and collection material. The bodybuilding example in [R03](../../sources/R03.md) also has Hulk triggering a reaction in Thanos. The comments under [R11](../../sources/R11.md) carry the family line on to later generations, but speculation must not be upgraded into an in-video relationship, still less overwrite other creators' versions.

## Characters and shared settings

Characters are looked up in one place in the [character files](../../characters/README.md); no duplicate character pages are created here. Shared settings such as parents' names and catchphrases can be referenced across universes; identity changes and cameos are noted with their version in the character files.

## Story patterns and gaps to fill

Early material mainly supports title-level leads such as the CP series, the witnessing episode and the borrowing-money episode; [R76](../../sources/R76.md) has since added the couple's domestic routine and Gamora's daughter label from episodes 2–4 of Kuangkuang Yuanyuan's series, without reconstructing every episode or settling on a single blood-relationship mechanism. See the [two-person relationship entry](../../relationships/灭霸与浩克.md). Contributions of actual examples of confessions, misunderstandings or relationship reveals are welcome; do not write plot from titles alone.

## Adjacent gag versions

The body pun and the Ant-Man comment in [R25](../../sources/R25.md) belong to another observable thread; there is not enough evidence to merge it into any creator's CP series. Sharing a character does not mean that the relationship, the location and the consequences are identical in every episode.

## Creator family lines and other favoured versions

[Gamora](../../characters/卡魔拉.md) is called the daughter in R76's series; that family identity is written back into the character and relationship files, and characters are not duplicated per universe. The shopkeeper Thanos who gives Hulk a free cake in [R82](../../sources/R82.md) and the hand-drawn grown-up sequel in [R83](../../sources/R83.md) belong to other creators; they are recorded separately and not merged into one shared family history.
