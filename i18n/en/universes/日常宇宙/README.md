## Delivery, shopkeepers and differences of identity

[R82](../../sources/R82.md) has a Spider-Man who earns pocket money by delivering at weekends and a Thanos who runs a small shop; [R74](../../sources/R74.md) has another shopkeeper, Fury. Work, pocket money and different treatment of customers drive everyday conflict, and the campus teacher or classmate identity is not inherited automatically. Look up [Spider-Man](../../characters/蜘蛛侠.md), [Thanos](../../characters/灭霸.md) and [Nick Fury](../../characters/尼克·弗瑞.md) separately.
