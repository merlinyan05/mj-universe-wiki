# Standard Universe / Avengers comedy

- Common scenes: the Infinity Gauntlet, the snap, assembling, combat preparation, ability counters
- World rules: the background is abilities, the Infinity Gauntlet, the snap and Avengers operations; this is a fan category and not equal to film canon.
- Version boundaries: universe categories can be layered and are not official universe numbers; specific creator versions are recorded separately.

Thanos' advance preparation and the Avengers' countermeasures escalate against each other, with Doctor Strange supplying plans; the comments pick up on Ant-Man's absence, other timelines and many endings. Campus phrases such as [R04](../../sources/R04.md)'s "tell the teacher" and "I don't want the gauntlet any more" can also be borrowed, but that does not make everyone a student.
