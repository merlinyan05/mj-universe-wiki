# Pranks and counter-pranks

- Type: a pattern shared across universes.
- Observations so far: partners pranking, asking for help and hitting back in the [Campus Universe](../校园宇宙/README.md).
- Cross-universe reuse: the setting and the leverage can be swapped while keeping the information gap and the attack/defence switch; specific creator versions are recorded with their own sources.

- Starting point: one side holds leverage or an information advantage.
- Picking up lines and anticipating: acquaintances exchange information first, predict each other's reaction, then expose it in public. [S1](../../sources/S1.md), [S5](../../sources/S5.md)
- Reversal: the one being pranked can also hold secrets, call in the parents or expose the other side. [R05](../../sources/R05.md), [S2](../../sources/S2.md)
- Audience feedback: some comments want Thanos to hit back, or explain the campus conflict as isolation; this cannot be used to prescribe one fixed motive for every creator. [R09](../../sources/R09.md), [R12](../../sources/R12.md)

This is a summary of the material, not proof that an original plot has already happened in some video.

## Direct video examples and temporary alliances

In [R42](../../sources/R42.md) the raccoon first exposes a love letter, Thanos asks Iron Man for help tracing the raccoon's private updates, and after both sides expose each other they face the parents being called together. Iron Man's help changes how information is distributed; the partner label alone cannot presuppose that he will never help Thanos.

[Fake systems and false authoritative answers](../校园宇宙/patterns/伪系统与假权威答案.md) adds the campus variant of "using a trusted authority to steer action"; [competition for public resources](../校园宇宙/patterns/公共资源争夺与能力交易.md) adds multi-person targets and trading. A video's mechanism does not equal a permanent moral label for a character.

## A warning that is not believed, and questioning that extracts a confession

- **A real alarm taken for a prank**: in [R79](../../sources/R79.md) a companion warns that the teacher is coming, Thanos keeps playing because he does not trust it, and the Ancient One really does appear. This can be summarised as a friend's credibility running out; it cannot be summarised as the friend never warning him.
- **The truth is not told in full, and the student gives up more on their own**: in [R73](../../sources/R73.md) the teacher keeps questioning, the student goes from online classes to hot pot, and the actual serious problem is an iron basin damaged earlier and hidden. The joke depends on props and actions that have already happened, not just on the teacher suddenly knowing everything.
- **The referee's attitude varies by creator**: R79's PE teacher protects the person being laughed at and the punishment carries over into the next IT class; another creator has the first person to jeer complain and Thanos end up criticised. The two arrangements are stored separately, and the teacher's personality is not unified.

## Multiple-choice questions and the reversal of moral talk

In [R74](../../sources/R74.md) the shopkeeper first offers the choice of spicy or not, then mocks the customer's choice of "not spicy" as an option that does not exist. This can be summarised as a fake choice question, and who asks and who answers must be kept.

In [R73](../../sources/R73.md) Rocket urges using a horror film to scare the doctor, but when he himself becomes the target of the film about to be played he objects that one should not scare a roommate. The change of target makes the same person change their tune; the dense jokes come from the shift in position, not from everyone taking turns repeating insults.
