# Superpowers and artifacts turned into household objects

- Type: shared across universes.
- Typical universes: standard Avengers comedy, campus, everyday life.
- Basis: [R16](../../sources/R16.md), [R19](../../sources/R19.md), [R21](../../sources/R21.md), [R22](../../sources/R22.md). Early records come from comments and titles respectively; direct video examples were added on 2026-11-09, and the actual verification scope is given in each source, with no shot-by-shot viewing of every work.

## How it unfolds

A power or artifact familiar to viewers appears → it is used for something very small and very ordinary → the way it is used or its consequence cheapens the artifact → someone else overrides it with an everyday object or social leverage.

The gap comes from what the power is used for: a tool for saving the universe is dragged into classmates' conflicts, eating and drinking, and everyday embarrassment.

## How to write with it, and boundaries

First choose which abilities this episode keeps, then make clear how they serve a small goal and why they invite a counter. Characters are still looked up in the [character files](../../characters/README.md); abilities must not be deleted wholesale just because the setting is a campus, and information causality must not be skipped just because there are superpowers.

The order of unfolding on this page is an editorial summary; writing it as the plot outline of a specific work still requires the original video's timestamps.

The hairdryer example in [R54](../../sources/R54.md) also turns a spell into a coin-retrieval service; see [competition for resources and trading](../校园宇宙/patterns/公共资源争夺与能力交易.md). A single transaction cost must not be written up as a unified spell economy.

## Hidden advantages switched off, and the conditions for using abilities

In [R73](../../sources/R73.md) Iron Man says ordinary people are not afraid of the cold, and after the suit's heating is switched off he asks for it back. This can be summarised as one round of short jokes: boasting → stripping away the advantage → immediate reaction; heating, offline films and power banks in the same series each have their own practical conditions.

The extreme-cold episode of [R80](../../sources/R80.md) uses a space-time freeze to keep things fresh; [R81](../../sources/R81.md) attributes a move failing to not having eaten breakfast. These are creator versions / episode conditions and do not turn one joke into a permanent power setting.
