# Name attacks and family-tree expansion

- Type: a pattern shared across universes.
- Typical case: classmates exposing, teasing and parents escalating in the [Campus Universe](../校园宇宙/README.md).
- Reusable scope: the [Standard Universe](../常规宇宙/README.md) and others can also ask "who is Mie Jianguo"; this states reusability and does not claim that new video examples have been checked.

1. Parents' names are first exposed as private information or as leverage; "who is so-and-so" repeats the name, and comments join in playing classmates. [R02](../../sources/R02.md), [R06](../../sources/R06.md), [R13](../../sources/R13.md)
2. The target of the prank shifts from the exposed person to the information provider, and viewers trace back Rocket Raccoon's family. [R02](../../sources/R02.md)
3. The chain is extended from the Jianguo and Zhiqiang generation to the Zihan and Yuxuan generation, and can also expand to a younger brother, a daughter and the grandparents. [R07](../../sources/R07.md), [R10](../../sources/R10.md)
4. The expansion of descendants and generations creates the escalation of "the whole family has been recognised"; where the evidence for a generation is unclear, the ambiguity is kept.

Same-name variants are listed side by side by source; a name appearing does not automatically prove a kinship. Character names are maintained in the [character files](../../characters/README.md).

## Chained complaints and the same name in different generations

[R47](../../sources/R47.md) pushes the complaint from students up to fathers, grandparents and higher authorities, and the same habit of naming names repeats at every level; in the end it meets a target with no corresponding father's weakness and the original attack fails. This is the escalation within one video example and does not require every name gag to run through all generations.

In the picture for [R48](../../sources/R48.md) the Fire Extinguisher is an elder brother, while in [R49](../../sources/R49.md) he is a grandfather in-video, and existing comments give yet other relationships. Names and kinship directions must be stored together with their creator; full names are maintained only in the [character files](../../characters/README.md), and different sources are not assembled into one single family tree.

Classmates also address the child directly by the father's name; a character's form of address does not mean the father and son are the same person. Original-work names, biological father/stepfather, homophone family terms and comment-made names must be kept apart.

## Checking back on the checker, and directional relative comments

In [R88](../../sources/R88.md) Thanos has a companion stall, traces Superman's father's name himself and then reveals it to his face; the new name is maintained in the [Superman file](../../characters/超人.md), and another family tree is not copied here.

The father/grandmother comments in [R75](../../sources/R75.md) have a clear direction, while the driftwood in [R84](../../sources/R84.md) is only a homophone of father and mother rather than a proper name. A single word, a one-off form of address and an explicit kinship label are handled separately.
