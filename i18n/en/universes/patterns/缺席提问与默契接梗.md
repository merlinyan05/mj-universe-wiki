# Absence questions and unspoken punchlines

- Type: shared across universes; it can also happen in the comment section.
- Core character: [Ant-Man](../../characters/蚁人.md).
- Basis: [R16](../../sources/R16.md), [R18](../../sources/R18.md), [R19](../../sources/R19.md), [R22](../../sources/R22.md).

## How it unfolds

Viewers or characters point out that a familiar person has not appeared → one short question brings back an existing joke → the other side only says "the usual place" or gives up explaining → everyone fills in the potential consequences.

The Bilibili creator of R16 and the Douyin creator of R18 both reply "the usual place" under "where did Ant-Man go / where's Ant-Man". These are creator replies on two pages, enough to record an elliptical punchline; the three words alone cannot establish the specific location.

## Variants

- A comment in R19 replaces the question with "where did the Wasp go"; absence and shrinking associations can be transferred to another person.
- A comment in R22 asks "where did Zeta go", and a reply changes "I don't want the gauntlet any more" into "I don't want Belial's badge any more", showing that viewers swap names and symbols across IPs. These are comment continuations and do not mean Zeta actually appears in this video.

## When it stops working

When viewers lack the prior knowledge, or when the location in this episode would change the outcome, clues have to be given. "The usual place" cannot be both an unspoken joke and a location proof strong enough to support every new plot.

It is a cross-entry with [Where did Ant-Man go](../../memes/蚁人去哪了.md); the character's location and the result of a single episode are not prescribed uniformly here.

## Leaving the car behind can also mean really looking for someone

In the racing video of [R87](../../sources/R87.md) Ant-Man's car is seen but he is not, and the location is never revealed; the outing episode ends with Hulk and Ant-Man missing, which drives the search that follows. Absence can be a complete suspense; not every case has to answer "the usual place".

[R75](../../sources/R75.md) also gives Ant-Man the function of relaying copy, so the character does not only carry the absence joke.
