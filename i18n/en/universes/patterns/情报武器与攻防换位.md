# Information as a weapon and swapped attack/defence

- Type: shared across universes.
- Typical universes: campus name attacks; it can also transfer to Avengers comedy.
- Basis: [R02](../../sources/R02.md), [R16](../../sources/R16.md), [R22](../../sources/R22.md). Includes editorial summaries of comments, not a survey of every work online.

## How it unfolds

One side holds news the other does not know → they confirm it privately with a partner and predict the reaction → they choose the moment to release it → the other side thinks they can only be laughed at → a new list, hidden information or another informed person appears → the original information position exposes its weakness, and attack and defence swap.

The key is "who knows what, and who is thought not to know". [Rocket Raccoon](../../characters/小浣熊.md) always knows the news first, which is a narrative position; making him lose requires him to misjudge whether he has already been traced back, and he must not be written as unable to read the situation for no reason.

## Video examples and comment versions

- An R22 comment says directly that "the information-position raccoon has been out-informationed", and compares the parents' list to the six Infinity Gems; the list becomes an effective weapon on campus.
- An R16 comment turns Rocket laughing as soon as he sees a parent's name into a "passive skill"; R02 has the gag that the skill needs nerfing. This gamifies a behavioural pattern, not a confirmed numerical skill system.
- [Thanos](../../characters/灭霸.md) can hit back once he has new information; [Doctor Strange](../../characters/奇异博士.md) has both a reporting version and a chivalrous version in the comment descriptions. Alliances must be explained episode by episode.

## Escalation and consequences

Escalation comes from new people being in the know or from new leverage, not from endlessly increasing volume. The consequence can be the jeerers falling silent, the list changing hands or classmates forming a temporary alliance; these are mechanism summaries, and which one has actually happened must be checked back against the source.

"The list is more useful than the Gems" is viewers comparing a joke, and cannot be rewritten as a physical power ranking. The existing [name attacks](姓名攻防与家谱扩写.md) entry continues to maintain the name-chain rules.

## How news is obtained, spread and followed up

[R40](../../sources/R40.md) has Rocket pinpoint the toilet precisely and call a crowd; [R41](../../sources/R41.md) explains Thanos' absence after being taken away by a parent. An information position can provide a location, mobilise others, and also fill in the aftermath.

[R42](../../sources/R42.md) has Iron Man trace the raccoon for Thanos; [R50](../../sources/R50.md) explains explicitly how a blocked update is obtained within the fictional plot. A change in attack and defence still needs a source of news, and cannot rely on unexplained omniscience.

## Wrong targets, reposters and sources of information

[R74](../../sources/R74.md) verifies the order of giving a hat to a teacher, Rocket misreporting the toilet target, and companions rushing over only to find the teacher. An information position does not guarantee getting the target right every time; how the hat's colour led to the misidentification is not directly explained, and the editor's explanation must not be written up as a verified process.

[R75](../../sources/R75.md) has Ant-Man, who is not blocked, repost the copy, providing another information chain. [R88](../../sources/R88.md) has Thanos arrange for companions to stall the checker while he traces the material himself, showing that obtaining a father's name can also be an action goal; the person exposed then counter-attacks, and the information side is not guaranteed to win.
