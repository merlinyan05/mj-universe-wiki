## Class monitor, responsibility and division of labor among companions

In the version by the 小新 creator in [R73](../sources/R73.md), 00:42 explicitly calls him the class monitor of the United States, and at 01:58.8–01:59.6 Captain America calls himself the class monitor and admits he has not taken the lead. When partners defer to each other he opens the door himself, and when blame is assigned he is willing to take it; these are concrete actions that support his sense of responsibility. He also takes on the task of carrying bowls and chopsticks during the hotpot shopping.

This is the identity of the [snowy-day dorm series](../universes/校园宇宙/作者系列.md); another creator has Doctor Strange serve as temporary class monitor, and the two cannot be combined into the same class being appointed at the same time.
