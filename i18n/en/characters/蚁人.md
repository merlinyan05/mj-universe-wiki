## Ways of appearing and everyday abilities

- **Absence itself can advance the joke**: the creators of R16 and R18 both answer "the usual place" when asked where Ant-Man is; this is a tacit understanding by omission, and no location is confirmed separately. [Absence-question pattern](../universes/patterns/缺席提问与默契接梗.md)
- **The target after shrinking can be very everyday**: the title of R19 explicitly says he gets into Thanos's cup to drink porridge and is discovered. The version "ability used to sneak into everyday containers" can be recorded; this round only glimpsed Thanos raising the glove at the end, so the full process and specific punishment are not written in. [R19](../sources/R19.md)
- **Do not fix his position in every episode**: the cup variant already shows that "where did Ant-Man go" does not mean hiding in the same place in every example.
