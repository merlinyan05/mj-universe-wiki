## Behavioral tools and relationship changes

- **Punchlines can turn into action**: a follow-up check of [R16](../sources/R16.md) has confirmed the in-video action of summoning JARVIS; in [R42](../sources/R42.md) he also helps Thanos trace back the raccoon's private updates. Technology becomes a tool that moves the conflict forward, and the real-world intrusion steps are not written in.
- **Partners do not always have the advantage**: parents' names can be traced back to him too; when the list changes hands, the prankster can become the one who has to clean up. [Comment summary of information offense and defense](../universes/patterns/情报武器与攻防换位.md)

[R42](../sources/R42.md) gives one episode in which he temporarily helps Thanos: after the raccoon exposes the love letter, Thanos asks Iron Man for help and he provides the tracing result. He can be summarized as that episode's source of ideas and technical intermediary, but "friendly partner" does not mean he permanently takes the same side in every episode.

In [R52](../sources/R52.md) he joins [JARVIS](贾维斯.md) and the Doctor in a fake-system prank; in [R50](../sources/R50.md) he first finds a way to obtain the blocked update and then organizes the follow-up questions. Cleverness and a love of pranks should be described through actions and are not directly escalated into omnipotence.

## Division of labor, shared resources and the conditions behind stubbornness

[R73](../sources/R73.md) assigns companion duties during hotpot shopping, and on snowy days uses the armor to warm companions and provide offline movies; resource advantages create negotiation and mutual dependence. In the snowball fight installment approx. 00:31–00:45, he says he is not afraid of the cold even as an ordinary person, then asks to restore the heating after it is turned off; the joke comes from the hidden condition being exposed.

[R78](../sources/R78.md)'s New Year installment approx. 00:14–00:15 snatches red envelopes and calls himself the phone king; the precise frame is Iron Man and is not misattributed to Rocket. He also asks Jarvis to check for fakes.
