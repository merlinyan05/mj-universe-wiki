## The protective-parent role and literalized names in comments

In R24, a viewer asks in the voice of a father who has been bullying his son at school, and the replies treat "Jianguo" as an achievement. This is comment role-play; R17 has a similar take with "the parent is here / he really did found the country". It can be recorded as a protective-parent reversal and a literalized name, but it does not prove his profession, regime or real historical experience. [R24](../sources/R24.md), [R17](../sources/R17.md)

## The warm version and getting along with classmates

In [R46](../sources/R46.md) he takes the child's classmates to harvest corn, arranges meals, and looks back on how young friends got along, showing tolerance when faced with jokes about his father's name. This adds the side of being willing to host children's friends; a single episode cannot be generalized into never getting angry, nor used to fix a single occupation.

[R51](../sources/R51.md) has him mistakenly learn school slang that the child explains, and bring it back when taking the teacher's call, becoming a link between family and campus conflict.
