## The protective-parent role and literalized names in comments

In R24, a viewer asks in the voice of a father who has been bullying his son at school, and the replies treat "Jianguo" as an achievement. This is comment role-play; R17 has a similar take with "the parent is here / he really did found the country". It can be recorded as a protective-parent reversal and a literalized name, but it does not prove his profession, regime or real historical experience.
