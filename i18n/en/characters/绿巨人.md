## Body jokes and CP are not forcibly merged

Comments on the new example R25 use "domineering and green-light" to continue the body pun, and some give different explanations of where Ant-Man sneaks in and what happens. This records only this chaotic branch and how viewers refer to it; it does not turn them into formal names, a fixed relationship or a definite ending, nor does it conclude that they are in the same worldline as the existing CP series just because the two appear together. ## Campus mutual help and audience positioning

[R41](../sources/R41.md) approx. 02:06–02:52 has Thanos borrowing a day-student card from the on-duty Hulk, adding practical help between classmates; this cannot be used to infer the card's identity, International Department affiliation or a fixed duty.

"Roommate big brother" is in the [R43](../sources/R43.md) comment character list; it is currently an audience summary and not confirmed as dorm head. Being in the same scene as Thanos also does not automatically enter the CP line.

## New evidence of family serials and dorm contrast

[R76](../sources/R76.md) adds in-video footage to the Crazy Yuanyuan serial: partner-style daily life, holding a baby in hospital, and daughter and mother forms of address, supporting that author's family interpretation; see the [relationship entry](../relationships/灭霸与浩克.md) and [Gamora](卡魔拉.md). Costume and gender presentation are recorded per author's version and are not directly merged with the other female Hulk character in the original work.

In the campus dorm line of [R73](../sources/R73.md), he can crush an iron basin or knead it into a ball, yet is afraid when he hears he might go to jail; friends compete to take responsibility. The concrete trouble-making, worry and mutual help add contrast beyond strength, and are not uniformly reduced to a simple giant or fixed dorm head.

In the snack-shop installment by another creator in [R82](../sources/R82.md), the Hulk in a dress gets free cake; a preference can be recorded, but this alone cannot establish marriage or the same world as the serial above.
