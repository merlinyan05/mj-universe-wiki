## How different versions are used

- The Thanos/Hulk CP already has titles and collection evidence for a continuous series; this does not determine who pursues whom in each episode or the specific relationship progress. [R03](../../../sources/R03.md)
- In the fitness situation he becomes the object of Thanos's attention; he cannot only serve the stereotype of "big so he must be dumb". [R11](../../../sources/R11.md)
- There is a version where a secret is discovered by Thanos, so the information position and the pranked can swap. [R05](../../../sources/R05.md)
- Universe-chaos comments also discuss Hulk in other timelines. [R04](../../../sources/R04.md)
- "Teacher Hulk" appears only in a recommendation tag and was not verified in the original video, so it does not replace other teacher versions. [R13](../../../sources/R13.md)

## Body jokes and CP are not forcibly merged

Comments on the new example R25 use "domineering and green-light" to continue the body pun, and some give different explanations of where Ant-Man sneaks in and what happens. This records only this chaotic branch and how viewers refer to it; it does not turn them into formal names, a fixed relationship or a definite ending, nor does it conclude that they are in the same worldline as the existing CP series just because the two appear together.
