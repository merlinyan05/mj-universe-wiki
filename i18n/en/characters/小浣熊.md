# Rocket Raccoon

- Aliases / search names: Rocket Raccoon, Rocket
- Branches involved: campus, parents'-name offense and defense
- Status: the scope of the material follows each source record; unverified editorial proposals are marked separately.

## Core positioning: the strongest information position

Rocket always knows the news before anyone else: whatever strange thing happens on campus, whoever gets their parents called in, whoever posts an update — before the others work it out, he already knows, and the news usually enters the plot through him.

"Strongest" describes his lead in information within the narrative, not combat power, and it does not mean he has no weaknesses. His role is not only to discover information but also to decide when to tell whom, moving the conflict to the next step.

On 2026-11-09 two direct examples were added: in the [toilet installment](../sources/R40.md) approx. 00:08–00:18, he accurately reports the floor and toilet stall where Thanos is and summons classmates; in the [day-student installment](../sources/R41.md) approx. 05:05–05:11, he explains to the Doctor the absence of Thanos after being taken away by his parents. The former is locating and spreading the news, the latter tracking and explaining the aftermath; he is not only responsible for reporting a name.

Earlier, [R26](../sources/R26.md) had only a title lead, and the scope checked at that time is kept; the new examples are not retroactively written as having been fully watched before. For the existing name reveal and comments, see [R02](../sources/R02.md).

## Behavior and interaction rules

- **Ahead on information**: while others are starting to discuss, he already holds the key news, rather than learning it at the same time as everyone else.
- **Banter with a partner**: he and Iron Man first comment in private, tease and predict each other, then bring the news into a face-to-face prank. [Partner collaboration example](../sources/S5.md)
- **Information drives consequences**: a reveal changes the other party's actions or reactions, and another layer of information can then be uncovered; record the exact order from the video examples.
- **He can also be traced back**: viewers turn around and ask for the raccoon's own parents' names, so an information advantage does not mean permanent safety. [Comment observation](../sources/R02.md)

This is a summary of the character's common function; these behaviors alone must not fix every version as "scheming" or "malicious". More specific motives, tone and counter-attack styles are welcome with video examples.

## New behavioral observations: passive punchlines and information misses

- **Names trigger mockery**: a comment in [R16](../sources/R16.md) summarizes laughing at a parent's name as a passive skill. This is a reaction habit after "knowing the news first" and does not replace the core positioning of the strongest information position.
- **Intelligence can fall half a step behind**: a comment in [R22](../sources/R22.md) explicitly says the information-position raccoon is countered by an information gap. Keep his ability to actively listen and judge timing, but allow that he may not notice the other side already has the list.
- **Advantages can be joked about as game mechanics**: the nerf meme in R02 and the passive meme in R16 are viewers describing behavioral rules as game mechanics; they cannot be used to give him a fixed cooldown, number of probes or unconditional omniscience.
- **Friendly-partner coordination needs private confirmation**: the existing partnership is kept; new comments also give Rocket and Iron Man their own punchlines instead of making the two take turns repeating. [R15](../sources/R15.md)

These new observations come from comments, not from newly checked in-video dialogue line by line. For reusable mechanics, see [information as a weapon and shifting offense and defense](../universes/patterns/情报武器与攻防换位.md).

## How different versions are used

- He controls and spreads key information such as relatives' names and records; viewers respond with jokes that his "intelligence ability" needs a nerf. [R02](../sources/R02.md) [S3](../sources/S3.md)
- The applicable scope of the positioning depends on the specific version; the existing material does not count how often he appears across all works online. [Editorial and verification boundaries](../sources/E01.md)
- He comments in private with Iron Man, trades punchlines and then pranks; information can be revealed in layers. [S1](../sources/S1.md) [S5](../sources/S5.md)
- He can be traced back himself; he is not an omniscient character without weaknesses. [R02](../sources/R02.md)

## Direct examples of action, reaction and contrast

- Locating information leads to on-site action and can also get parents called in; he does not always stay out of it after leaking the news. [R40](../sources/R40.md)
- He stirs up trouble after discovering someone else's love letter, and his own private updates are also traced back by Iron Man, after which he and Thanos expose each other. [R42](../sources/R42.md)
- "Strongest information position" is a narrative summary of these information-leading behaviors, not a global ranking certified by comparison in the videos.

## Purchasing tastes, personal resources and information misses

- In the hotpot installment of [R73](../sources/R73.md) approx. 02:49–03:03, he chooses tangyuan and insists on his own taste, which adds that author's preference for sweets. Later companions also complain about the overcooked tangyuan, so he is not only present to leak information.
- In the accountability installment of the same series approx. 02:09–02:16, the teacher says the Guardians' captain is his family member; the direction of kinship and the name are unknown, so it is not directly registered as his father.
- [R74](../sources/R74.md) has another creator make him report the toilet target as Thanos, and the companions find out it is the teacher. What is verified is the false information and its consequences; how the hat caused the misidentification is not directly explained.
- In the horror installment of [R73](../sources/R73.md), he first encourages scaring the Doctor, and after becoming the target of the "raccoon killer" proposal, he switches to arguing that roommates should not be scared, showing a contrast in stance that changes with the target.
