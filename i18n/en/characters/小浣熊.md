- Aliases / search names: Rocket Raccoon, Rocket

## Core positioning: the strongest information position

Rocket always knows the news before anyone else: whatever strange thing happens on campus, whoever gets their parents called in, whoever posts an update — before the others work it out, he already knows, and the news usually enters the plot through him.

"Strongest" describes his lead in information within the narrative, not combat power, and it does not mean he has no weaknesses. His role is not only to discover information but also to decide when to tell whom, moving the conflict to the next step.

"The toilet has a golden python, he knows first" and "whoever gets their parents called in, he knows first" are editorial examples illustrating this behavioral rule, not verified events from the original videos. Another public video title mentions "the golden python and chocolate sauce", but this round did not see in-video evidence that Rocket learns it first, so the title cannot be used to complete this event. [R26](../../../sources/R26.md) For the existing parents'-name reveal and the comment jokes about his intelligence ability, see [father's name reveal and raccoon chain](../../../sources/R02.md).

## Behavior and interaction rules

- **Ahead on information**: while others are starting to discuss, he already holds the key news, rather than learning it at the same time as everyone else.
- **Banter with a partner**: he and Iron Man first comment in private, tease and predict each other, then bring the news into a face-to-face prank. [Partner collaboration example](../../../sources/S5.md)
- **Information drives consequences**: a reveal changes the other party's actions or reactions, and another layer of information can then be uncovered; record the exact order from the video examples.
- **He can also be traced back**: viewers turn around and ask for the raccoon's own parents' names, so an information advantage does not mean permanent safety. [Comment observation](../../../sources/R02.md)

This is a summary of the character's common function; these behaviors alone must not fix every version as "scheming" or "malicious". More specific motives, tone and counter-attack styles are welcome with video examples.

## New behavioral observations: passive punchlines and information misses

- **Names trigger mockery**: a comment in [R16](../../../sources/R16.md) summarizes laughing at a parent's name as a passive skill. This is a reaction habit after "knowing the news first" and does not replace the core positioning of the strongest information position.
- **Intelligence can fall half a step behind**: a comment in [R22](../../../sources/R22.md) explicitly says the information-position raccoon is countered by an information gap. Keep his ability to actively listen and judge timing, but allow that he may not notice the other side already has the list.
- **Advantages can be joked about as game mechanics**: the nerf meme in R02 and the passive meme in R16 are viewers describing behavioral rules as game mechanics; they cannot be used to give him a fixed cooldown, number of probes or unconditional omniscience.
- **Friendly-partner coordination needs private confirmation**: the existing partnership is kept; new comments also give Rocket and Iron Man their own punchlines instead of making the two take turns repeating. [R15](../../../sources/R15.md)

These new observations come from comments, not from newly checked in-video dialogue line by line. For reusable mechanics, see [information as a weapon and shifting offense and defense](../../../universes/patterns/情报武器与攻防换位.md).
