## Appearance reference

As of 2026-11-10, 13 of the 21 character files provide external reference entries; Mie Jianguo, MJ and Fat Spider-Man, JARVIS, Doctor Fate, Nick Fury, Loki, Superman and Gamora (8 files) still need dedicated references. The table above links directly to each character's reference section, covering three-view sheets, multi-view sheets, concept art and costume material from films, games, comics and fan versions; the material type, specific version and scope checked are written in each file, and generic concept art must not be called a complete three-view sheet.

This is an index of external links; no third-party images are copied into the repository, and it is not a complete MJ campus character asset pack. Public availability does not permit reposting, commercial use or uploading to generative models, and this repository's CC license does not cover linked images or third-party character rights.
