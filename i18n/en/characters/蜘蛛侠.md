## The phone's original owner and the weekend delivery examples

In the first half of the phone video in [R77](../sources/R77.md), the red-and-blue Spider-Man called MJ is the original owner of the phone; after taking turns borrowing it, he ends up with little playtime himself. Do not misremember the character just because the video title says Thanos. [R82](../sources/R82.md) approx. 00:52–00:54 also has the red-and-blue Spider-Man called MJ explaining that he delivers on weekends to earn money for spicy strips and instant noodles.

These two entries add campus shared resources and an everyday part-time job identity; they do not merge every MJ into Fat Spider-Man because of the abbreviation, nor do they infer a transformation ability from a sudden change in body size in the generated footage.
