# JARVIS

- Aliases / search names: JARVIS; homophone transcriptions are not treated as formal aliases.
- Branches involved: campus technical pranks, everyday life and cross-franchise cameos.
- Status: recorded by creator and episode; not all film abilities are assumed by default.

## Behavior and manner of speech

- **An assistant that executes orders**: Iron Man makes a request, the system voice responds and moves the action forward; the teacher network, private-update traceback and fake systems appear in [R16](../sources/R16.md), [R42](../sources/R42.md) and [R52](../sources/R52.md) respectively.
- **A bridge for information sources**: [R50](../sources/R50.md) explicitly arranges for him to obtain blocked content, supplying the causality of how Iron Man knows; this does not mean every example must use the same explanation.
- **Calm responses create absurd consequences**: the reply in [R62](../sources/R62.md) is "put it back", while [R66](../sources/R66.md) is "spread it evenly". The two punchlines are kept separately per original video; a popular line is not transplanted into another work.

"Reliable technical assistant" and "misunderstands the request" are different uses; he cannot be written as always reliable or always wrong. The character's response is often an off-screen system voice; whether he has a human form and who can hear him must still be checked per example.

## Universe and relationships

The request/execution relationship with [Iron Man](钢铁侠.md) is visible in the examples above; a single assist does not establish that he always serves every classmate. For cross-franchise examples see the [cameo notes](../universes/跨宇宙与客串.md).

## Everyday function and explicit limits

In [R73](../sources/R73.md), when the network is down on a snowy day and the base station only supports calls, JARVIS does not conjure the internet; the armor still has offline movies. A technical assistant can help within the available conditions and need not be omnipotent.

[R78](../sources/R78.md) helps identify whether clothes are genuine in the New Year episode; [R81](../sources/R81.md) judges an outside identity based on the local same-face character still being in the building. Both are bridges for how specific information enters the action.
