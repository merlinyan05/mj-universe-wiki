- Status: the scope of the material follows each source record; unverified editorial proposals are marked separately.

## How different versions are used

- Campus emo branch: he posts a serious, sad update and is quoted by acquaintances the next day, who pick up keywords and give him nicknames; the emotion and the loss of face form a contrast. [S1](../sources/S1.md) [S2](../sources/S2.md)
- He denies, gets annoyed, tells the teacher or asks for help; he can also turn the information around and prank others, so he is not fixed as the permanent victim. [R05](../sources/R05.md) [R12](../sources/R12.md)
- Some creator versions have a Thanos/Hulk CP; this cannot be applied to every branch. [R03](../sources/R03.md) [R11](../sources/R11.md)

## New observations on personality and reactions

- **Serious sadness and acquaintances' teasing hold at the same time**: comments in R15 and R17 keep turning images such as the sting and the shell of the copy into nicknames, which shows that what he expresses is seized on by people who know him, rather than every line being deliberately funny. [Copywriting imagery pattern](../universes/patterns/文案意象字面化与评论续演.md)
- **Information can be used as a weapon**: comments in R22 compare the power of him obtaining the list to the Infinity Gems; when he gets a chance to trace back, he can also take the initiative, so he does not have to be bullied all the time. [R22](../sources/R22.md)
- **Feeling wronged does not equal being simply stupid**: comments in R24 include both sympathy and people playing along in the tone of "I saw it long ago, I was just teasing my classmates". The latter is comment continuation and cannot be used to conclude that the video has confirmed he was pretending all along. [R24](../sources/R24.md)

Sympathy, mockery and expectations of revenge coexist in the same comment section, so this is not counted as a consistent preference across the internet.

## Privacy protection, proactive tracing and friendship

- In [R50](../sources/R50.md) he blocks Iron Man himself and then posts; another plot has the "bad friend" obtaining the content anyway, so "blocking is useless" cannot be written as a rule that works for no reason.
- In [R42](../sources/R42.md), after his love letter is exposed, he takes the initiative to ask Iron Man to trace it back, showing that he is capable of asking for help, taking revenge and switching sides. [R52](../sources/R52.md) also has a fantasy counterattack version in which he accepts a fake system mission.
- Examples such as friends pooling instant noodles after his birthday plan falls through, and his nostalgia for old friends after graduation, add mutual help and reluctance to part to his relationship with classmates. [R55](../sources/R55.md), [R56](../sources/R56.md)

"Wanting to protect face / hoping to be recognized" is an organizing reading of the above behaviors, not a psychological setting shared by all creators; he is not compressed into a fixed victim who only cries.

## Using the prank routine himself and different everyday identities

[R78](../sources/R78.md) has a version in which he actively posts a bait update, pretends to be angry the next day, and successfully trades for spicy strips. This sits side by side with the earnest, sad emo examples; not all sadness is turned into calculation. In the New Year video, a friend kindly brings back dirty clothes, which instead exposes his father's story.

[R82](../sources/R82.md) also has a snack-shop owner identity: he quotes prices to ordinary customers, sells instant noodles, and gives Hulk cake for free. [R83](../sources/R83.md)'s hand-drawn "Later" depicts graduation, design work, shelved drawing and family life; it is the author's adult sequel, not a shared future of all campus versions.

The partner and parent-child forms of address in the family serial of [R76](../sources/R76.md) are in [Thanos and Hulk](../relationships/灭霸与浩克.md) and are not combined with other creators' shop-owner, adult-partner and child versions into one family.
