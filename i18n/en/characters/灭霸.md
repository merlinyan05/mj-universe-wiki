## How different versions are used

- Campus emo branch: he posts a serious, sad update and is quoted by acquaintances the next day, who pick up keywords and give him nicknames; the emotion and the loss of face form a contrast. [S1](../../../sources/S1.md) [S2](../../../sources/S2.md)
- He denies, gets annoyed, tells the teacher or asks for help; he can also turn the information around and prank others, so he is not fixed as the permanent victim. [R05](../../../sources/R05.md) [R12](../../../sources/R12.md)
- Some creator versions have a Thanos/Hulk CP; this cannot be applied to every branch. [R03](../../../sources/R03.md) [R11](../../../sources/R11.md)

## New observations on personality and reactions

- **Serious sadness and acquaintances' teasing hold at the same time**: comments in R15 and R17 keep turning images such as the sting and the shell of the copy into nicknames, which shows that what he expresses is seized on by people who know him, rather than every line being deliberately funny. [Copywriting imagery pattern](../../../universes/patterns/文案意象字面化与评论续演.md)
- **Information can be used as a weapon**: comments in R22 compare the power of him obtaining the list to the Infinity Gems; when he gets a chance to trace back, he can also take the initiative, so he does not have to be bullied all the time. [R22](../../../sources/R22.md)
- **Feeling wronged does not equal being simply stupid**: comments in R24 include both sympathy and people playing along in the tone of "I saw it long ago, I was just teasing my classmates". The latter is comment continuation and cannot be used to conclude that the video has confirmed he was pretending all along. [R24](../../../sources/R24.md)

Sympathy, mockery and expectations of revenge coexist in the same comment section, so this is not counted as a consistent preference across the internet.
