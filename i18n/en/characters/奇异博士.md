- The universe-chaos version can take on counter-measures; "I have seen many endings" is used by comments to explain other timelines. [R04](../sources/R04.md)

## New observations on behavior, relationships and forms of address

- **His stance changes per episode**: a comment in R16 contrasts this episode's "being a real friend" with an earlier "reporting someone for bringing a phone". This is a viewer's retrospective on the series and supports keeping both the loyal and the snitching leads; it does not fix him as a permanent informer, nor does it directly claim both episodes were fully verified. [R16](../sources/R16.md)
- **His abilities can enter the campus story**: a follow-up check on 2026-11-09 of [R16](../sources/R16.md) at about 00:40–00:48 shows him teleporting in to rescue people and helping Thanos get away; this is not only a comment lead, and the student identity does not mean he has lost his spells.
- **Academic titles are mocked by downgrading them**: "Strange Vocational School" and "Strange Junior College" are used in R16 comments as nicknames for the doctor; they are not parents' names, nor proof of his real education in that universe. Family-chain items such as "doctor/master" must be kept separate from his own nicknames.

## Author differences in behavior and stance

In [R44](../sources/R44.md) he accepts the temporary class-monitor duty, records names according to how people speak, and protests only make the record escalate; [R16](../sources/R16.md) also has him helping a friend get away. Conscientious / rule-following and mutual help among classmates can be written separately; he is not fixed as either a perpetual informer or a perpetual cover-up.

The grade dialogue in [R43](../sources/R43.md) supports that author's top-student version; nicknames such as "Doctor Junior College" still do not equal a real academic record.

## Motives for reporting and everyday resource ability

In the second half of the phone video in [R77](../sources/R77.md), the Doctor asks to borrow the phone and is refused; after the teacher checks the phone, he reports it so that he will not give it back for his own play, which supports that author's revenge-reporting motive. In the first half the phone's original owner is the Spider-Man called MJ, so it is not all attributed to Thanos just because the title says Thanos.

[R80](../sources/R80.md)'s milk-frog cold-wave line lets the Doctor use space-time freezing to preserve supplies; this is that series' resource ability and cannot be generalized into an infinite-warehouse rule for all creators. [R81](../sources/R81.md)'s other series also attributes a failed move to the enemy not having eaten breakfast; a single-episode joke is not a permanent power setting.
